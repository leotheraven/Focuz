# Artwork production

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, using the user-provided pastoral painting as a style reference. Original generated art was sliced into game sprites and resized for bundling; no procedural plant drawing is used.

Assets live in `MamHabit/Assets.xcassets`. 32 plant frames, 4 backgrounds, 5 accessory images. Pots from the draft accessory atlas were discarded at the user’s request.

## Prompt set

### art-sprout
Use case: stylized-concept. Production transparent PNG botanical game sprite sheet for Focuz. Subject: a majestic lush green broadleaf oak tree, irregular airy canopy, layered lime green sunlit leaves and deep teal shadows, beautifully textured brown branching trunk. Four growth frames in an EXACT 2 by 2 grid, equal square cells on a square canvas: top left small newly sprouted plant with 3 leaves, top right beautiful young sapling, bottom left half-grown juvenile tree, bottom right magnificent full mature tree. Each plant completely contained within its cell, generous 10% empty padding, no overlap, aligned roots at 90% of each cell height. The mature tree fills 80% of its cell. The young plants are genuinely smaller and have fewer leaves. Painterly hand-painted anime countryside art, gouache brush texture, intricate organically clustered foliage, sunlit highlights from upper left, soft warm summer atmosphere, elegant natural proportions. Match the attached landscape's brushwork and colors. Isolated plant sprites with TRUE TRANSPARENT background and a tiny natural grass tuft at the roots; no scenery, no pots, no labels, no text, no frames, no grid lines, no faces, no geometric vector blobs, no cast shadow rectangle.
